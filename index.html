How the numbers work (assumptions)
  • All figures are fictional and made up for teaching. Same inputs always give the same results; there is no randomness.
  • Demand = 470 × (6 ÷ price)1.2. Higher prices lose guests; lower prices win them.
  • Capacity = staff hours × 10 orders per hour. Staff cost = hours × $20.
  • Natural wait = 2 + 7 × (demand ÷ capacity)³ minutes, max 25. Your line limit caps the wait; if the natural wait exceeds it, that share of guests leaves.
  • Happiness starts at 88, minus 3 per wait minute over 4, minus 4 per $1 above $6, minus a stock-out penalty, plus a small bonus for extra staff. Kept between 20 and 98.
  • Repeat visits: orders scale by 1 + 0.004 × (happiness − 80), limited to −15% / +10%.
  • Waste: under 10% waste you run out of items and lose 1.2 × (10% − waste) of orders.
  • Costs: food $1.55 per order served, plus the wasted share; card fees 3% of revenue; rent and utilities $650/day.
  • Limits: orders can't exceed 105% of staff capacity; price $4–$9; staff 24–100 hours; waste 2–30%; line limit 3–15 min.
  • The wait uses demand before the happiness effect, a deliberate simplification.

Pour Decision Café is a fictional business. All numbers are illustrative, not real café data.
